Sourcing

Where to buy, what importing really costs, and every quote in one place. Ask for a sample before any bulk order, and compare landed cost (price + freight + GST you can't claim), not the headline price.

Before you pay a supplier

•Pay only through the platform or to a business account that matches the GST/company name on the quote.Advance payment to a personal UPI for a "factory" is a common way new sellers lose money.
•Order a sample and test it the way a buyer would.Most returns come from "not as shown" and quality, which a sample catches.
•Get the invoice with GST shown if you are GST-registered.Without it you cannot claim the GST you paid.
•Check the product is allowed on your channel.Branded goods without authorisation, and some categories, need approval or documents.

Wholesale markets and hubs

Buying in person lets you check quality and bargain. Places widely known for: Delhi Sadar Bazaar (general goods, festive items, packaging), Moradabad (brass and metalware), Jaipur (block-print textiles, jewellery), Panipat (home furnishings), Tirupur (knitwear), Ludhiana (woollens and hosiery), Surat (fabrics and sarees), Khurja (ceramics).

Import
AlibabaImport

Often cheaper per unit, but you need an Importer Exporter Code (IEC) from DGFT, and you pay customs duty, freight and clearing charges. Minimum order sizes are usually set for bulk buyers. Put real numbers in the calculator before deciding; at ₹10,000 domestic stock usually wins.

Import or buy in India?

Enter the supplier's price and today's rates. Duty and IGST depend on the product's HS code; look them up in the customs tariff on ICEGATE or ask a customs broker.

Packaging

Couriers charge the higher of actual and volumetric weight (L×W×H ÷ divisor). Each marketplace sets its own divisor and weight slabs; check its shipping fee page and change the divisor to match.

GST

Look up the HSN code and rate for your exact product on the CBIC GST portal, or ask your CA. The rate you save here is used in the margin score and invoices.

GST invoice for direct sales

For orders on WhatsApp or your own site. Marketplaces issue the invoice for their own orders. Same state: CGST + SGST. Different state: IGST.

Label cropper

Turn the A4 label/invoice PDF from your seller panel into labels that fit a 4×6 thermal printer. Drag a box around the label on page 1; the same box is cut from every page. The file stays on your device.

Listing writer

Write a title, bullet points and description from facts you give it. It only uses what you type, so put in real sizes, materials and what's in the box. Check every line before you publish; a wrong claim causes returns.
