Suppliers

Every quote in one place. Ask for a sample before any bulk order, and compare landed cost (price + freight + GST you can't claim), not the headline price.

Add a quote

Before you pay a supplier

•Pay only through the platform or to a business account that matches the GST/company name on the quote.Advance payment to a personal UPI for a "factory" is the most common way new sellers lose money.
•Order a sample and test it the way a buyer would.Most returns come from "not as shown" and quality, which a sample catches.
•Get the invoice with GST shown if you are GST-registered.Without it you cannot claim the GST you paid.
•Check the product is allowed on your channel.Branded goods without authorisation, and some categories, need approval or documents.
